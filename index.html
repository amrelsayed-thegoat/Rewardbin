The Problem

Rubbish is often left on the ground in schools, parks and public places. Normal rubbish bins do not give people a reason to use them. RewardBin gives people a reward for making the right choice.

How It Works

Three easy steps.

1

Throw

Put your rubbish into the RewardBin. Sensors inside the bin detect when rubbish is placed inside.

2

Earn

The RewardBin gives you a token for correctly throwing away your rubbish.

3

Redeem

Save your tokens and exchange them for rewards, food or special prizes.
